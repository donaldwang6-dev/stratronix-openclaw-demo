Why OpenClaw is rock-solid

Auto retry
Network blips, rate limits, 5xx — smart exponential backoff (2s, 30s, 5m). No babysitting, auto-archived on completion.
Resumable
Cursor-based incremental sync. Resumes from last checkpoint — no re-runs, no missing rows. Failures pinpoint to the exact line.
Full observability
Every step logs timing, state, and error codes. Alerts fire the moment something drifts. Native integrations with Feishu / DingTalk.
