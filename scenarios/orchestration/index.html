SCENARIO 02

Scheduled orchestration

7 crons scattered across 4 machines. One silent failure breaks the whole stream. OpenClaw turns them into a single DAG — with explicit dependencies, retries, and alerts, and one command to see the entire pipeline.

Cron collapse
7→1
7 cron jobs → 1 DAG
Before
7 crons · 4 machines
# crontab -l (fragmented)
0 2 * * *  sh /jobs/etl_orders.sh
0 3 * * *  sh /jobs/etl_ads.sh
30 3 * * * sh /jobs/etl_cs.sh
0 4 * * *  sh /jobs/etl_merge.sh
0 6 * * *  sh /jobs/report_pdf.sh
0 7 * * *  sh /jobs/report_send.sh
*/15 * * * * sh /jobs/heartbeat.sh
  • · Failures discovered by humans, after the fact
  • · Dependencies live in tribal knowledge
  • · Changing one time means SSH-ing into 4 machines
After
1 DAG · single source of truth
flow:
  - id: etl_orders
    next: [etl_merge]
  - id: etl_ads
    next: [etl_merge]
  - id: etl_cs
    next: [etl_merge]
  - id: etl_merge
    next: [report_pdf]
  - id: report_pdf
    next: [report_send]
retry: exp(2s, 30s, 5m)
  • · Failures retried automatically with exponential backoff
  • · Dependencies live in the DAG, visible at a glance
  • · One command (openclaw run daily) to see the whole pipeline
▸ Auto retry
Network blips, rate limits, 5xx — smart exponential backoff, no human babysitting.
▸ Observable
Every node logs timing, state, and errors. Alerts fire the moment something drifts.
▸ DAG clarity
Topology is readable at a glance. Edits stay inside the file, no tribal knowledge required.
